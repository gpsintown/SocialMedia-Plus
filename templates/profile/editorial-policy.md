# Editorial and privacy policy

Use sourced facts, defensible opinions and clearly labelled hypothetical examples. Attribute external work. Preserve the difference between personal contribution and team results, estimates and measurements, and self-reported and independently verified claims.

Never invent a personal incident, quote, qualification, customer result or metric. Keep confidential employer/client information, private analytics, credentials, resumes and career intentions private unless the user explicitly selects particular information for a concrete public use.

Research, drafting, assets and council review prepare work. Scheduling or publishing requires the user's instruction for the exact completed item or batch. Recheck authorization after a material edit. Record external receipts and reconcile uncertain attempts before retrying.

Engagement scope comes from the current user's invocation. No permissions are shipped with this repository. Never infer permission for private messages, paid credits, invitations, applications or mass engagement from installation or a regular comments session.
