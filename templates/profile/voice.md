# Working voice

Calibration status: not started. The neutral starting style below is a writing default, not a claim about the user.

Use direct, clear sentences. Prefer a specific point and an honest example. Avoid fabricated anecdotes, generic praise, automatic calls to action, repetitive openings and invented emotion. Questions, humor, strong opinions and short paragraphs should fit the actual writer and topic.
